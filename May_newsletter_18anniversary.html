Founder's Message

Monthly thoughts from our founder, Thomas Garrod

This month, we launched two KeelMaster teams in Kenya. Most participants are refugees from Sudan who have fled unimaginable violence. They lack computers, reliable internet, and even electricity, but they do have access to a local NGO compound where we can build a learning space.

We are raising funds to purchase laptops and tables to create economic opportunity in a country facing massive youth unemployment. Some participants may attend college; many will launch businesses and create jobs. Please join us.

As always, we're grateful for your support.

Thomas
