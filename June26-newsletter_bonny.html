KeelMaster Kenya: Real-Time Progress Report

Thanks to the incredible support of our community, we have raised $2,034 toward our current goal, with $2,000 still needed to fully support the first KeelMaster cohort in Kenya.

So far, we have successfully purchased 6 computers to help students begin their digital learning journey. In addition, our team has completed 2 out of 9 KeelMaster courses, while active development is underway for the remaining 7 courses.

As the program grows, so do the technical needs behind it. Building courses, hosting learning platforms, and supporting program tools are increasing our cloud hosting and infrastructure costs each month. These resources are essential to delivering a stable and high-quality learning experience for our students.

Every contribution directly helps us expand access to technology, complete the learning curriculum, and continue building opportunities for young refugees in Kenya.

A Day in the Life: KeelMaster Student in Kenya

Dongangen David Kuol fled conflict and displacement in South Sudan, eventually arriving at the Kakuma refugee camp. Despite hunger, limited resources, gender-based violence, and overwhelming hardship, she remained determined, completing high school in 2024. Living with her unemployed mother and 5 siblings, she sees education as her path to a better future. Her story is one of resilience, hope, and the quiet determination of a girl who refused to give up.

Reflections

For 18 years, KeelWorks has worked toward one belief: human potential exists everywhere, but opportunity does not. Across communities, cultures, and continents, we've learned that lasting impact is not created through charity alone; it is built through empowerment, trust, education, and shared purpose. These lessons come from thousands of conversations, volunteers, learners, mentors, and supporters who shaped the KeelWorks journey. They continue to guide us as we work toward a more peaceful and equitable world.
